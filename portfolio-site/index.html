about

A fresh graduate who teaches himself the rest

I am Yhurie Paul P. Cainglet, a fresh BSIT graduate and an aspiring Software Engineer. I did not come into programming already knowing how.

I taught myself, starting from small console exercises up to desktop applications with login flows, data tables and printable receipts. My school's projects gave me the problems; the hours after class gave me the answers. Six of those projects are in this portfolio, and every one of them I can walk you through line by line.

Four I built on my own. Two were team coursework projects with classmates, and those cards say who was on the team.

education

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

BSIT · St. John Paul College of Davao

what school gave me

The problems, not the answers. Coursework handed me requirements and deadlines; it did not hand me working login flows or data models, so those I had to build and rebuild until they held.

mindset

Most of my projects are the third or fourth version. My first point-of-sale screen duplicated a cart row on every click. My C# record app let edits vanish the moment I switched views. I did not fix those by finding a tutorial that already had the answer — I found them because something looked wrong, traced it, and repaired it.

learn  →  build  →  break it  →  read my own code  →  fix it  →  build again

I would rather show you a working project with an honest list of its bugs than a polished description of something I cannot open. Every limitation listed in this portfolio is one I found myself.
